The problem

Backtests flatter.

Almost any strategy can be tuned until the past applauds. Then it meets tomorrow and dies quietly, taking real money with it.

Tweak enough parameters and any chart turns green — that is not an edge, that is memorizing history.

The hard part of finding alpha was never the finding. It is knowing which finds are real — before you trade them.

What AlphaNode does

Evolution, not opinion.

breed

Formulas are small programs built from price, volume and funding — 200 per generation, 30 generations, ≈ 6,000 candidates a round — mutated and crossed like organisms, round after round.

test

Every candidate trades a fee- and funding-aware simulator over years of history on 50 liquid crypto pairs, from daily bars down to 15 minutes. Weak ones are culled; nothing is hand-picked.

refine

New rounds warm-start from your champions and mutate around what already works; every few rounds the search explores from scratch so it never gets stuck on one idea.

keep

Survivors land in your library with their full record. You press one button and read the leaderboard — the machine does the rest.

The honesty rules

A formula cannot pass
by memorizing the answers.

two windows

History is split. A formula's score is the worse of two separate windows — shining on one and failing the other counts as failing.

locked test

A third slice is held out and locked. The search never sees it, never selects on it — it only grades the finished formula at the end, pass or fail.

forward track

Champions then trade live closed bars on paper, appended one by one with a deliberate lag — no acting on a bar that has not finished, no editing yesterday.

real costs

Every simulated trade pays exchange fees and funding. An edge that only exists before costs never reaches your screen.

In the box

From first launch to your own pipeline.

one button

Start mines; the live page shows rounds, generations and finds as they happen — on the desktop or in any browser for a server node.

portfolio

Combine your best formulas into one portfolio, ranked by the honest TEST column, and read a PDF report of how it would have traded.

signal api

A local JSON endpoint serves this hour's target weights for your basket — wire it into your own execution when you decide to go live. Your keys, your risk, your code.

no babysitting

The node fetches its own market data, tracks its champions forward and keeps mining unattended — laptop, desktop or a $20 server.

Yours, locally

Runs on your hardware.
Finds stay yours.

private

Mining and forward tracking read public market data only — no exchange API keys, no cloud. The only thing that leaves the machine is a licence check.

everywhere

Windows, macOS, Linux — or a headless server node with one Docker compose file and a live status page.

unique

Each install mines from its own seed: no two nodes walk the same path, so your library is yours in both senses.

sealed

On disk, mined formulas are encrypted to your account — a copied library file is useless on someone else's machine. Your key opens them; nobody else's does.
