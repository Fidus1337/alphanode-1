Why trust the numbers

    
Anti-overfitting is the whole point.

    
Any search can print a beautiful backtest. AlphaNode is built so that
    a beautiful backtest is hard to fake — the discipline is wired into the engine.

    

      

        
Fitness = worst segment

        
A formula scores as its weakest period, not its best. Robust across
        regimes beats brilliant in one lucky year.

      

      

        
TEST stays locked

        
The held-out window never influences selection. It's computed once, shown as
        an honest out-of-sample estimate.

      

      

        
Diversity, not clones

        
Correlation dedup, a complexity penalty and an activity filter keep the
        library varied, simple and actually trading.

FAQ

    
Fair questions.

    

      

        Does AlphaNode guarantee profits?
        
No — and be suspicious of anyone who says otherwise. Every number shown is a
        hypothetical backtest. What AlphaNode guarantees is discipline: a locked held-out
        window, robustness-first fitness, and built-in paper trading so you can prove — to
        yourself first — whether an alpha survives contact with the future. Nothing here is
        investment advice.

      

      

        What do I need to run it?
        
A Linux or Windows 10+ machine and an internet connection for market data. A few
        CPU cores are enough — no GPU.

      

      

        Do my formulas or data ever leave my machine?
        
No. Search, simulation and the library are fully local. The only outbound traffic
        is market data downloads from Binance — nothing else ever leaves the machine.

      

      

        Which markets and timeframes?
        
Binance USDT-M perpetual futures, up to 150 pairs, on 15m / 1h / 4h / 1d bars.
        Funding rates are part of both the simulation P&L and the formula language, so
        carry-style alphas are searchable too.

      

      

        How is this different from a backtesting library?
        
A backtester scores strategies you write. AlphaNode generates them: it searches a
        formula space that contains the classic hand-written signals and everything between,
        while the engine's fixed machinery — sizing, vol targeting, inertia, fees, funding —
        keeps every candidate realistic.
